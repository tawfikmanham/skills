## Practice log

| Date and practice day | Project, screen, and user task | First pass → rewrite | What improved or needs work | Next exercise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record a row after each practice session. Link to the session notes if available. Keep the original comment and coach feedback in those notes or below the row’s date in this file. Add the rewrite and its scores to the same entry when ready.

Use the coach rubric to score **evidence, reasoning, communication, and next step** out of 2 each. Leave pending scores open and mark criteria you cannot assess as unscored.

## Example checkpoints for 30 days

Start with the first unaided critique as your baseline. Adjust the dates and exercises to fit your schedule and what you want to improve.

| When | Exercise |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Describe one visible detail, explain how it might affect the user’s task, and suggest a way to check. |
| Day 7 | Replace a vague comment such as “cluttered” with the specific labels, spacing, or grouping that led to it. |
| Day 14 | Pick a step in a short flow and explain how one design choice might affect the user’s next action. |
| Day 21 | Turn one prediction into a check you could run in a prototype or usability test. |
| Day 30 | Critique a new screen of comparable complexity before getting feedback. Compare the first draft with your baseline using the same criteria. |

## Review your progress

Use the difference between a first pass and its rewrite to see what improved with coaching in that session. Compare unaided first passes on similar tasks to see whether the skill carries over.

At each checkpoint, point to one strength and any recurring gap supported by the entries. Reference the dates or session notes. If the entries are too few or too different to compare, describe what you observed and choose the next exercise from that. Update the summary and exercise at the top.
