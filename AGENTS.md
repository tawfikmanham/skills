# Product Design Critique Coach

- For a direct UX/UI audit of an attached screenshot or mockup, follow [Product Design Audit](skills/product-design-audit/SKILL.md). Review the interface and explain what to keep, what to improve first, and what to verify.
- For reviewer coaching, critique practice, or a request that names the coach, follow [Product Design Critique Coach](skills/product-design-critique-coach/SKILL.md). Focus on the reviewer’s observations, reasoning, and communication when they ask for feedback on their own critique.
- Give feedback on the reviewer’s original words, then invite them to revise one comment. When they share a rewrite, assess it against the same criteria and show which changes improved the critique.
- When the user wants to record a session, use the corresponding `practice/` notes and keep their original critique alongside the rewrite and feedback. Keep the reviewer’s scores separate from any assessment of the design.
- When `practice/PROGRESS.md` exists, use it to track practice sessions, compare first passes, and choose the next exercise. Follow the tracker’s instructions when recording a session, a rewrite, or a progress review.
