# Design Critique Coach

- For screenshot critique or reviewer coaching, follow [Design Critique Coach](skills/design-critique-coach/SKILL.md). Focus on the reviewer’s observations, reasoning, and communication when they ask for feedback on their own critique.
- Give feedback on the reviewer’s original words, then invite them to revise one comment. When they share a rewrite, assess it against the same criteria and show which changes improved the critique.
- When the user wants to record a session, use the corresponding `practice/` notes and keep their original critique alongside the rewrite and feedback. Keep the reviewer’s scores separate from any assessment of the design.
